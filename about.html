✳ MY PERSONAL INTRODUCTION

I enjoy turning ideas into digital experiences.

Hello! I'm Sohail Channa, a Full Stack Developer with an interest in modern website development. My goal is to understand how complete web experiences are created, from the first visual idea to the functionality that makes an application useful.

I enjoy working with HTML, CSS and JavaScript because they provide the essential building blocks for creating interactive websites. I also explore Node.js to understand server-side development and how frontend applications communicate with backend systems.

My interest is not limited to writing code. I also pay attention to presentation, organization, readability, responsive layouts and the details that make a website easier to understand and navigate.

I see development as a continuous process. There is always another concept to explore, another challenge to understand and another opportunity to improve the quality of a digital product.
